# Engagement Rules

1. Do not assume the client has already defined the right product.
2. If the evidence does not answer a question, mark it as unknown.
3. Distinguish stakeholder desire from approved business rule.
4. Prefer a thin vertical slice over a broad platform.
5. Mock external complexity, not the core decision.
6. Your demo should show one complete workflow.
7. Be explicit about what you intentionally did not build.
