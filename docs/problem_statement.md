# Client Problem Statement: Placement Day Control Room

During major campus placement days, hundreds of candidates, recruiters, interviewers and rooms have to be coordinated at the same time.

Interviews often start late. Candidates wait without knowing when they will be called. Recruiters sometimes wait for candidates while other candidates wait for available interviewers. Room changes and delays are coordinated manually through spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages and phone calls.

The Placement Head believes an AI-powered system could help and has asked your FDE team to:

> Build a system that makes placement day run more efficiently and improves the candidate experience.

Your job is to determine:
- what the actual problem is,
- who you are solving for,
- which operational decision matters,
- what information is trustworthy,
- what the smallest useful product should be,
- what can be mocked,
- what must use real data,
- and what you can demonstrate end-to-end today.

## Important rule
Do not jump directly to building a dashboard. First define the user, decision, data, workflow and MVP slice.
